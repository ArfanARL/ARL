03 / HEALTHCARE AT SCALE

From the clinic to the
provincial core.

Healthcare information is only useful when it connects. Our provincial HMIS links facilities, district hubs and a secure core around a unified patient record — with AI as governed decision support, never a silent replacement for clinical judgement.

  • Longitudinal record — one patient history across facilities
  • Connected diagnostics — LIS, RIS, PACS and blood-bank traceability
  • Standards-based — ICD-11, SNOMED CT, LOINC, HL7/FHIR
  • Surveillance — ICD-11 disease signals for public-health teams
