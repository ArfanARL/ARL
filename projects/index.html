How we label our work

Operational (reported)
Deployment and usage figures are system-reported by the project owner and have not been independently audited. Definitions and measurement dates should be confirmed before they are relied upon.
Active initiative
An ongoing institutional initiative. No performance, accuracy or outcome figures are claimed.
Proof of concept
Design or proof-of-concept work. It is not presented as a production deployment, and no measured operational outcome is claimed.
Completed project
A completed project delivered through Agentic Research Labs (Pvt) Ltd for the client named in the project context. Figures are as reported for the project.
