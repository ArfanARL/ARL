WHO WE ARE

Applied AI, engineering and domain understanding.

Established in 2007, Agentic Research Labs (Pvt) Ltd is a Pakistan-based technology and applied-research company focused on intelligent digital systems for enterprise, public-sector and essential-service environments. Our work spans AI and agentic systems, healthcare and enterprise platforms, data intelligence, geospatial technologies, systems integration and digital infrastructure.

We approach technology through the full problem lifecycle: understanding operational needs, researching viable approaches, designing the architecture, engineering and integrating the solution, and supporting readiness for real-world use.

Registered business-object clauses

Current (modified) business objects

The principal line of business of the company shall be to research, develop, and deploy Large Language Models (LLMs), agentic AI solutions, and specialized healthcare IT software; to operate AI and software development centers, providing end-to-end IT consultancy, custom software engineering, and data processing services; to impart technical training in AI and software systems; and to trade, import, export, and license related software, hardware, and computing infrastructure, subject to necessary regulatory permissions.

Original business objects

The principal line of business of the company shall be to establish and run data processing centers, computer centers, software development centers, offices and to provide consultancy and data processing software development services, both application packages and operating systems and other services, and to impart training of electronic data processing, computer software and hardware to customers and others and to buy, sell, export, import of software, hardware and establishment of incidental infrastructural facilities, subject to permission of relevant authorities.

RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

Fit for purpose.
Explainable. Governed.

We believe AI and automation should be fit for purpose, explainable to their users and governed throughout their lifecycle. In sensitive domains such as healthcare and public administration, technology should support accountable professionals and established institutional processes — not silently replace them.

01

Human-centred

Technology should strengthen professional judgement and improve the experience of the people who use it.

02

Secure by design

Security, privacy, access control and accountability belong in the architecture from the beginning.

03

Evidence-led

We value clear requirements, measurable outcomes, careful evaluation and transparent limitations.

04

Built to operate

We design for maintainability, integration and the realities of long-term operational environments.
